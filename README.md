# THE WORLD TODAY — Tuesday 24 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261124`: sum up
24 November 2026's world news in one image.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) draws a newspaper front
page — masthead, date, a lead story and seven more stories across
regions and fields — and writes it to `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024
PNG, deterministic: two runs produce identical bytes).

Stories (all reported on 24 November 2026):

- Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia chief executive; shares jump 31%
- Ukraine says its naval drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury
  off Benghazi, Libya
- Overnight Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia kills two, wounds ~40
- Israeli airstrikes in Gaza kill Hamas internal security chief
  Naeem Abu Nahel and six others
- China opens an investigation into Politburo member Chen Jining
- Factory fire in Banten, Indonesia kills 26, dozens missing
- COP31 climate talks end with no fossil-fuel deal
- About 175 migrants missing after contact is lost with a
  Europe-bound boat from Senegal

Sources: Reuters, Associated Press, BBC, The Guardian, Xinhua.

Run it:

    MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
